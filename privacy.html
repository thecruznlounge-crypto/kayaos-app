Last updated: [date]. This policy explains what Kayaos (kayaos.space) collects, why, and who it is shared with. Kayaos is operated by [business name], [address], [contact email].

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Your cards and reminders: titles, dates, notes, checklists, and any photos or videos you attach.
  • Work records: jobs, rates, shifts, and payments you enter.
  • Fuel and vehicles: vehicles, fill-ups, odometer readings, and receipt photos you add.
  • Price reports: fuel prices you submit for a station, and optionally a photo of the price sign. Submitted prices are shared with other users.
  • Location: your approximate location is used on your device to find nearby stations and show weather. Coordinates are rounded to about 100 meters. We do not keep a history of where you have been.
  • Saved places: cities you add for the time and weather strip, and stations or places you favorite.
  • Device data: settings, and cached data stored on your device (localStorage and IndexedDB). This stays on your device unless you sign in and sync.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your data between your devices, show weather and nearby stations, read fuel prices from photos you choose, generate voice reminders, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with (service providers)

  • Supabase: hosts your account, synced data, and uploaded files.
  • Anthropic: when you use the price-sign reader, the photo is sent to Anthropic to read the prices. Anthropic does not receive your account details through this feature.
  • OpenAI: when you use natural AI voices, the reminder text is sent to OpenAI to create the voice.
  • Open-Meteo: receives the coordinates needed for weather and city search.
  • OpenStreetMap and Overpass: receive the area you search to find nearby stations. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap or Esri.

Each provider handles data under its own terms. We only share what each service needs to do its job.

Photos

Photos you add are stored in your private storage folder. Price-sign photos you choose to share can be viewed by other signed-in Kayaos users, since they support the price report. Do not photograph people or private information you do not want shared.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete cards, shifts, payments, fuel logs, and photos in the app. To delete your account and all of its data, contact [contact email]. We will delete it within [30] days.

Your choices

You can use the app without signing in. Local-only data stays on your device. You can turn off location in your phone settings, and the app will still work with cities you add yourself.

Children

Kayaos is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your data is protected by access rules so that only you can read your own records. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
